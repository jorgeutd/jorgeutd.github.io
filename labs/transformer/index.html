About & experience

Staff / Principal AI Engineer.
Model depth. Systems ownership.

Jorge Grisman · Worthington, Ohio

I'm Jorge Grisman, a Staff / Principal AI Engineer based in Worthington, Ohio. I design, build, and deploy AI systems across model fine-tuning and domain adaptation, agent orchestration, retrieval, and cloud and on-device inference.

My work spans the full delivery path: adapting encoder and generative models, quantizing and serving them under memory and latency constraints, integrating them into applications, and building evaluation, tracing, and release systems. I stay hands-on while leading architecture and technical decisions across product, ML, and platform teams.

I've delivered across technology, industrial distribution, insurance, and healthcare, including NLP, retrieval, and recommendation capabilities within a healthcare navigation platform serving 15M+ members. That figure describes platform reach. My engineering career began in process optimization in 2013 and grew into applied ML, model systems, and production AI leadership.

How I work

Start with the task and a measurable baseline. Design for latency, memory, cost, and failure. Evaluate model behavior and the complete workflow. Keep versions, traces, and release evidence connected so a team can reproduce a result, investigate a failure, and improve the next release.

A small model, with inspectable math. One pre-norm block, sinusoidal positions, two heads, GELU, no dropout, identity normalization scale and zero bias. This decoder recomputes the full sequence; it does not use a KV cache. Fixed illustrative weights have never been trained, so probabilities do not measure language understanding.

Model specification & visual references

16 vocabulary entries · hidden width 8 · head width 4 · feed-forward width 16 · no projection biases · untied output weights · layer-norm ε = 10⁻⁵. Deterministic initialization seed 73. Green and terracotta vector cells encode positive and negative values; the attention matrix encodes weight from 0 to 1. Sampling uses a reproducible pseudorandom draw, reset with the input.
